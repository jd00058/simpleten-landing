Consulting engagements

AI consulting services built around your needs.

SimpleTen consulting engagements are designed to help you understand where AI can make a difference, plan realistic next steps, and guide implementation without forcing you into predetermined solutions.

AI Readiness & Opportunity Assessment

Understand where AI and automation can make the greatest impact.

We evaluate your current operations, software, workflows, bottlenecks, and opportunities to identify the highest-value areas for AI adoption. You'll leave with a prioritized roadmap of practical next steps.

AI Strategy & Roadmap

Build a realistic plan for adopting AI systems over time.

We help you develop a practical AI strategy that considers your goals, capacity, budget, and existing technology. Our roadmaps prioritize quick wins alongside longer-term transformation.

Process Mapping & Automation Planning

Map your current workflows and identify what should change.

We document how work actually moves through your business, identify inefficiencies and dropped handoffs, and design automation workflows that connect the right tools and people.

Implementation Oversight & Fractional AI Advisory

Ongoing guidance as you adopt AI and automation systems.

We provide fractional AI leadership and implementation oversight to help guide vendor selection, validate technical decisions, review progress, and keep your AI adoption on track.

AI Policy & Responsible-Use Guidance

Build practical AI policies your team can actually follow.

We help organizations develop clear AI usage policies, data privacy guidelines, responsible AI practices, and employee guidance that balance opportunity with appropriate caution.

Vendor Evaluation & Technology Selection

Choose the right AI tools and vendors for your business.

We help evaluate AI software, automation platforms, and implementation partners so you can make informed vendor decisions without getting locked into the wrong solution.

Our process

The SimpleTen four-step process.

Every SimpleTen engagement—whether consulting, implementation, or both—follows the same practical framework.

01

Uncover

Uncover the problems. We start by understanding your business, bottlenecks, leaks, opportunities, and the outcomes you're trying to achieve.

02

Map

Map the solution. We identify the highest-leverage opportunities, prioritize realistic next steps, and design systems around your existing operations.

03

Install

Install our engine. Whether we implement the automation ourselves or guide your chosen vendor, we help connect the right tools, workflows, and people.

04

Track

Track your results. We measure what's working, identify new opportunities, and help refine your systems as your business grows and AI capabilities evolve.
