The problem

You Didn't Start a Business to Sort Email.

Every day, real inquiries land between vendor invoices, newsletters, receipts and cold pitches. The owner becomes the filter, replies slip to tomorrow, and a lead that waits long enough calls someone else.

Lead leak

A new inquiry sits under a stack of newsletters.

By the time you see it, they've already booked with whoever answered first.

Time leak

You check the same inbox all day, just in case.

Scanning for fires isn't work. It's busywork that eats the hours you'd rather spend with customers.

Sales leak

Cold-email replies pile up unsorted.

Interested, not now, unsubscribe and out of office all look the same in a crowded inbox.

Customer leak

A client's urgent question looks like every other email.

The one message that needed an answer today gets the same attention as a receipt.

The solution

Simple Inbox Reads Every Email, So You Only Read the Ones That Matter.

Simple Inbox answers the same few questions about every message: what kind of email is this, how urgent is it, and is this a real lead? Clear answers get labeled automatically. Leads and client questions get a draft reply you can edit and approve. Anything it isn't sure about goes to a review queue for a person.

Under the hood, a structured decision model can only answer from the options we set up with you, and it reports how sure it is on every answer. It can't write the email for you, and it can't send one. Your judgment stays in charge; the sorting just gets out of its way.

Our philosophy

We don't automate people away. We free them from the busywork so they can do the work that matters.

No one gets into a service business to do admin. You do it to solve problems and make people's lives better. We make the busywork simple, so you can focus on service.

FAQ

Straight Answers.

Will it ever send an email on its own?

No. Simple Inbox can create drafts, and that's where it stops. Sending is switched off in the code itself, not just in a setting. You, or someone on your team, review and send every reply.

How accurate is it?

We won't quote an accuracy number before we've measured it on your email. That's what the pilot is for: we label a sample together and show you exactly where it agreed with you and where it missed. Even a confident answer can be wrong, which is why only reversible actions like labels and drafts happen automatically.

What happens when it isn't sure?

Every answer comes with a confidence level. Anything below the line we set from your pilot goes to a review queue for a person instead of being guessed.

Where does my email go?

To make each decision, the fields it needs (sender, subject and message text) are sent to a hosted decision-model provider. We strip what isn't needed, review the provider's data terms with you before go-live, and log every decision. If your email can't leave your systems, tell us during Map and we'll talk through a local option and its trade-offs.

Do I have to change email providers or how I work?

No. Simple Inbox works inside the inbox you already use. Gmail and Instantly come first, and we scope anything else during Map before you pay for setup.

Does it replace my office manager or assistant?

No. The goal is to remove the sorting, so the people on your team spend their time on customers instead of scanning an inbox.

What does it cost?

The pilot is $497, credited toward setup if you continue. After that, setup starts at $1,500 and the monthly plan is $297 for up to 3 inboxes. You'll get a fixed quote after Map.
