What does SimpleTen do?

SimpleTen is a Sacramento AI consulting and automation business. We help service businesses connect lead follow-up, sales, operations, reporting, and team knowledge using practical workflows and AI where it adds value.

How much does AI automation cost?

Your initial business assessment is free. Implementation depends on the workflow, connected tools, data quality, and support required. We agree on scope and pricing before a paid build begins.

Do I need new software?

We start with your existing tools. After reviewing their capabilities and limitations, we recommend additional software only when it is needed for the agreed workflow. Any ongoing software costs are discussed during scoping.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Our focus is service businesses: home services, professional services, agencies, field-service teams, and growing local operators. The best starting point is a repeatable process with a clear owner and a measurable problem.

How long does it take to get started?

The assessment identifies a starting point. Implementation timing is then based on access to your tools, the number of connections, and testing needs. We scope focused workflows first and agree on milestones before building.

How do you handle sensitive information and AI mistakes?

We define access limits, approved information, review points, and exception handling for the workflow. Important decisions stay with people. Any industry-specific requirements need to be reviewed before implementation.

Do you serve businesses outside Sacramento?

Yes. We serve the greater Sacramento region, including Roseville, Elk Grove, Folsom, Rancho Cordova, Davis, and Rocklin. Contact us about your location and the best way to collaborate.
