The problem

Your business may be losing money in places you can’t see.

A missed call. A lead nobody followed up with. An estimate that went cold. A customer who never got asked for a review. Across hundreds of calls, customers, jobs, and transactions, these small problems become expensive. SimpleTen finds those leaks—then builds AI and automation systems to close them.

01

Lead Leak

Stop losing opportunities you’re already paying for.

Missed calls. Slow responses. Forgotten web forms. After-hours inquiries. Leads sitting untouched. SimpleTen can automatically capture, respond to, qualify, route, and follow up with new opportunities.

02

Sales Leak

Don’t let good opportunities quietly disappear.

Estimates without follow-up. Prospects who said “let me think about it.” Old leads sitting inside your CRM. We build follow-up systems that keep opportunities moving until customers book, buy, or tell you no.

03

Time Leak

Stop paying people to do work software can handle.

Duplicate data entry. Manual scheduling. Repetitive customer questions. Copying information between systems. We connect your tools and automate repetitive administrative work.

04

Customer Leak

The relationship shouldn’t end when the job does.

Reviews never requested. Referrals never asked for. Previous customers forgotten. Maintenance and renewal opportunities missed. SimpleTen helps automate reviews, referrals, reactivation, retention, and ongoing customer communication.

What does SimpleTen do?

SimpleTen is a Sacramento AI automation company helping local service businesses capture leads, automate follow-up, connect tools, reduce repetitive admin, and improve customer communication.

Is this only for companies with big budgets?

No. Big companies have AI departments. Small businesses have SimpleTen. We scope practical systems around existing tools, business value, and manageable first steps.

Do all automations use AI agents?

No. Many steps are better handled by straightforward rules and integrations. AI is useful when classification, drafting, intake, knowledge retrieval, or natural conversation adds value.

What types of businesses do you work with?

SimpleTen focuses on local service businesses: home services, professional services, agencies, field-service teams, and growing operators across Sacramento and nearby communities.

How do you avoid replacing people?

We automate tasks, not purpose. The goal is to give people more capacity to serve customers, solve problems, build relationships, and do higher-value work.
