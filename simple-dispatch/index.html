The problem

Your Dispatcher Shouldn't Have to Be a Human Search Engine.

The numbers in your business are usually clear. The language isn't. Customers describe problems their own way, techs write notes in shorthand, and the same person ends up in your system three times. Someone in the office untangles all of it by hand, every day.

Time leak

"It's making a weird noise."

Is that a repair, a maintenance visit or an emergency? Someone has to read every request and decide.

Customer leak

The wrong crew shows up.

A job routed to the wrong skill set means a second trip, a frustrated customer and a lost afternoon.

Sales leak

Same customer, three records.

Bob Smith, Robert Smith and a new phone number. History gets split and follow-ups go to the wrong place.

Time leak

Tech shorthand never matches the catalog.

The note says one thing, your parts list says another, and someone spends twenty minutes matching them.

Sales leak

You find out a critical part is gone when the truck's in the driveway.

Stock looked fine on paper until three open jobs needed the same part.

Customer leak

The notes knew first.

A tech wrote that the customer was frustrated, or asked about a new unit. No one read it in time.

The solution

A Second Set of Eyes on Every Job. One That Only Suggests.

Simple Dispatch makes the judgment calls that rules can't: what kind of job this is, which crew fits, whether this customer already exists, which catalog part the tech meant. It puts each one in front of your dispatcher as a suggestion, with how confident it is.

The split matters. Your software keeps counting stock, totals and schedules the way it always has. A structured decision model only reads the prepared facts and picks from the options you allow, such as your real crews, your real catalog, or "no match". Your team approves, and only then does anything change.

Our philosophy

We don't automate people away. We free them from the busywork so they can do the work that matters.

No one gets into a service business to do admin. You do it to solve problems and make people's lives better. We make the busywork simple, so you can focus on service.

How it works

Uncover. Map. Install. Track.

The same four-step process we use for every SimpleTen install, aimed at your dispatch desk.

01

Uncover

We sit with your dispatcher or office manager and find where requests, duplicates and parts questions eat the day.

02

Map

We define your job types, crews and skills, what counts as an emergency in your trade, and your catalog. Then we label about 100 past jobs together.

03

Install

We connect Simple Dispatch to your field-service software, add your crews and catalog, and give your dispatcher a review queue of suggestions to approve or edit.

04

Track

We measure how often suggestions are accepted without edits, review the misses with you, and tune the rules.

FAQ

Straight Answers.

Will it dispatch jobs automatically?

No. Every suggestion goes to your dispatcher to approve or edit. Automatic routing for low-risk jobs is something we'd only discuss later, if you ask for it and the numbers support it.

How accurate is it?

We won't quote a number until we've measured it on your jobs. That's the point of the pilot: we label about 100 past jobs with your team and show you where Simple Dispatch agreed and where it missed. A confident suggestion can still be wrong, which is why a person approves every one.

Does it work with my field-service software?

Yes, connecting it is our job. As part of setup, SimpleTen connects Simple Dispatch to the field-service software you already run, plus your CRM or spreadsheets if you use them. Suggestions show up where your dispatcher already works. We confirm the connection details during Map, before you pay for setup.

Will it replace my dispatcher?

No. It takes the sorting, searching and matching off their plate so they can focus on customers and crews. Your dispatcher stays in charge of every decision.

Which trades is it for?

Service businesses that dispatch crews: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, appliance repair, pest control, landscaping and property maintenance. We pilot one trade at a time so the job types fit your work.

Where does my job data go?

Each decision sends only the facts it needs to a hosted decision-model provider. We review the provider's data terms with you before go-live and log every decision. If your data can't leave your systems, we'll talk through a local option and its trade-offs during Map.

What does it cost?

The pilot is $1,500, credited toward setup if you continue. After that, setup starts at $3,500 per location and the monthly plan is $497 per location for up to 10 techs. You'll get a fixed quote after Map.
