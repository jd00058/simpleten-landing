The challenge

AI is changing jobs. Workers need skills, not fear.

Technology is shifting how people work. Employees, small business owners, and job seekers need practical AI skills and confident literacy—not hype, fear, or tools they'll never use. SimpleTen helps organizations provide AI training that prepares people for real work.

01

The AI Skills Gap

Workers don't know where to start with AI.

Most people know AI exists but don't understand how it could help them work smarter, communicate better, solve problems faster, or make better decisions. They need clarity and confidence.

02

Fear of Replacement

Employees worry AI will automate them away.

Many workers see AI as a threat to their jobs. SimpleTen's philosophy is freeing people from busywork so they can focus on work that matters—not replacing purpose with automation.

03

Impractical Training

Generic AI courses don't translate to real work.

Employees need training connected to their actual jobs—customer service, admin work, sales, operations, problem-solving, communication, and decision-making—not abstract theory.

04

Employers Need Solutions

Organizations don't know how to upskill their teams.

Employers, workforce boards, colleges, and public agencies want practical AI training for their people but don't have internal capacity or the right partners to deliver it.

Training programs

Workforce development programs built for your team.

SimpleTen offers practical AI training programs designed for the workers, small businesses, and organizations that make our communities work. We focus on skills people can use immediately—not buzzwords or tools that will be obsolete in six months.

AI Literacy & Fundamentals

Understand what AI is, how it works, and where it fits.

Foundational AI literacy for employees, managers, small business owners, and community members. Covers practical AI concepts, responsible use, common tools, and how AI is changing work.

Practical AI Tools for Frontline & Admin Staff

Use AI to work smarter, not harder.

Hands-on training for customer service teams, administrative staff, and frontline workers. Learn practical AI tools for communication, scheduling, data entry, customer follow-up, document work, and problem-solving.

Automation Skills for Small Business Owners

Understand where AI can help your business grow.

Targeted training for small business owners and managers. Covers lead response automation, CRM systems, customer communication, operations, and how to evaluate AI vendors and tools.

Train-the-Trainer Programs

Equip your team to deliver AI training internally.

Train workforce development professionals, educators, and internal trainers to deliver practical AI literacy and skills training to their own teams and communities.

Custom Cohort Programs

AI training designed around your organization's needs.

Custom training programs for employers, workforce boards, colleges, and agencies. We design curriculum, delivery formats, and outcomes around your specific workforce, industry, and goals.

AI Workshops & Lunch-and-Learns

Practical AI sessions for teams and organizations.

Single-session and multi-session workshops on AI basics, automation opportunity, practical tools, and responsible AI adoption. Available on-site or virtually for teams, conferences, and events.

Delivery formats

Training that fits your team and schedule.

SimpleTen workforce development programs are available on-site, virtually, and in cohort-based formats designed to meet your organization where you are.

01

On-Site Training

In-person training at your workplace or facility. Best for teams who benefit from hands-on instruction and direct interaction with trainers.

02

Virtual Training

Live online training sessions delivered via Zoom or your preferred platform. Accessible for distributed teams, remote workers, and regional cohorts.

03

Cohort-Based Programs

Multi-week programs with structured curriculum, peer learning, and practical projects. Ideal for workforce boards, colleges, and organizations building long-term AI capacity.

04

Workshops & Short Sessions

Focused 60–90 minute sessions on specific AI topics. Great for lunch-and-learns, conference sessions, and introductory training.
